# GSoC Preparation

This document is specifically for Google Summer of Code preparation. General open-source skills and contribution logs live in the separate [Open-Source Journey](../Open%20Source/README.md).

## Goal

Prepare for GSoC 2027 by identifying a suitable organization/project, understanding its community and codebase, making meaningful contributions, and preparing a realistic proposal that follows the program's official rules and timeline.

## Preparation principles

- Verify program dates, eligibility, organization lists, and rules using official GSoC sources when they are published; do not assume dates from earlier years carry over.
- Choose organizations based on project fit, active maintainers, a clear contribution path, and genuine interest—not just perceived low competition.
- Study the actual codebase and participate in project discussions before proposing a large idea.
- A proposal should reflect conversations with the community, realistic scope, milestones, risks, deliverables, and evaluation criteria.
- Contributions help demonstrate familiarity and collaboration, but no number of PRs guarantees selection.
- Keep the workload sustainable alongside college.

## Proposal notes template

For each candidate project, record:

1. **Problem:** What user or project problem needs solving?
2. **Current behavior:** What does the codebase do today?
3. **Proposed outcome:** What concrete behavior or deliverable will change?
4. **Scope:** What is included and explicitly excluded?
5. **Implementation plan:** Which modules, APIs, data structures, or integrations may be involved?
6. **Milestones:** Break work into reviewable stages with deliverables.
7. **Testing and evaluation:** How will correctness and success be measured?
8. **Risks and alternatives:** What could block progress, and what is the fallback?
9. **Community alignment:** Which maintainer discussions or project docs informed the proposal?
10. **Official requirements:** Confirm formatting, eligibility, dates, and submission rules from the current GSoC site.
